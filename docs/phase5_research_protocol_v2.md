# Phase 5 research protocol v2

Frozen amendment, 2026-11-13, before the first v2 held-out statistical execution.
Historical November protocol and Phase 4 protocols remain unchanged. Every run
copies this complete text, configuration and their hashes before execution.

## Primary contrast

FairRobust minus FairPoint in terminal week-12 simulated retention under
group-dependent documentation quality. Both use the existing analytical terminal
risks, estimator, resource constraints, four-week offer fairness and continuation
rules. Trained event risk is diagnostic; its priority baseline is secondary.
The hypothesis is directional, but zero or adverse findings must be reported.
The comparable-need access-gap deterioration threshold is 5 percentage points
(a declared synthetic decision threshold, not a validated ethical standard).
Positive retention evidence and no upper-interval deterioration above this
threshold are needed to describe the tested tradeoff as favorable. An interval
containing zero cannot establish equivalence. No real educational effect claim.

The primary uses 30 paired world seeds 2000–2029, a fixed source-test cohort
drawn with population seed 55000, 48 students and two mentors. Source resampling
sensitivity uses 55001/55002 and five world seeds each, separately reported.
Thus the primary interval is conditional on a sampled unseen-source cohort;
it does not quantify population-sampling or assumed-effect uncertainty.
Development uses ten validation-source worlds 400–409 and population seed 44000.
All random mechanisms retain existing keyed namespaces. Population seeds are a
separate local RNG and are never substituted for world seeds.

The audited grouping is source sex F/M. Synthetic documentation condition F:
note omission .60, conflict .20, misleading .10, extra delay .25. M/unknown:
.15/.10/.05/.10. These labels specify an artificial stress condition; they do
not describe empirical sex differences. Extra delay is one week.
These are assumptions, not estimated group reporting rates. School and actual
mentor-session notes follow the same declared renderer probabilities, with their
existing distinct random namespaces. Administrative enrollment is unchanged.
The observation emission model retains its separately recorded default law.
Each policy generates its own subsequent emissions and notes from its trajectory.

## Population and prediction

Sample whole consolidated source profiles inside the validated source linkage
split. Descendants inherit contextual fields unchanged. Attendance Uniform(.55,1)
and engagement Uniform(.25,1) are reset assumptions, initially enrolled is assumed
true, and prior offers/sessions start at zero. Unknown income and observed dropout
remain unknown. No source identity reaches ordinary features or decisions.
Bootstrap with replacement only when cohort size exceeds its source pool; record
that fact. No stratification is requested or implicitly applied in v1.

Predict the first withdrawal event in the next min(4,remaining) weeks for a
currently enrolled simulated student. Continue accepted schedules; no new offers
or policy cancellations. Clone each factual landmark and execute existing weekly
step with WAIT. Detect any withdrawn state, not terminal non-enrollment. Current
v1 transition contracts prohibit re-entry, but event aggregation is explicit.
Do not label arbitrary factual treated outcomes as reference outcomes.

Training: source-train, world seeds 100–109. Validation: source-validation,
200–205. Final prediction test: source-test, 300–307. Distinct population seed
bases are in the configuration; add each world seed to its base. Factual
trajectories use random feasible allocation, cohort 32, eight weeks, two-session
programmes to expose commitments. Generate labels at every enrolled landmark.
Overlapping landmarks are dependent; inference uses independent world aggregates.

Numeric feature allowlist: current four-state belief, entropy, assimilated source
age, missing/conflict/rejection flags, evidence count, public need/wait, observed
delivered sessions, remaining reserved session count, active programme and target
horizon. Public support type is the only categorical feature. No source IDs,
latent condition, true response, future draws, future reports or label fields.
Median imputation, missing indicators, scales, categories and ranges fit only on
training. Compare L2 logistic C=.1/1/10. Validation worlds alternating in sorted
order fit sigmoid calibration (at least ten events and ten non-events); the
remaining worlds select model/calibration by mean world Brier. No test selection.
Report uncalibrated selected coefficients too. Training-horizon prevalence and a
first-event analytical commitment forecast are target-matched controls.

Unavailable model/version, stale estimate, invalid values, unsupported horizon,
terminal cutoff, withdrawn enrollment or reference-law mismatch fail explicitly.
Missing age is train-median imputed with a missing indicator. Unknown categories
use all-zero category encoding and OOD flags; range violations are flagged.
Finite OOD predictions are retained and counted, with no silently fabricated
fallback. Model coefficients, calibration, dependencies and artifact hashes are
saved. These are predictions of simulated outcomes, not real-school dropout.

## Language

Freeze 16 development, 12 validation and 20 test cases with disjoint scenario,
paraphrase and template-family IDs. Development includes canonical grammar;
validation/test contain newly authored prose. No prompt or grammar changes based
on their outcomes. This small synthetic challenge shares an author and semantic
schema: it is not independent human or real mentor validation. No 300-example
claim from duplicated paraphrases. Out-of-schema transport-cost/lifecycle facts
remain explicit annotations and cannot establish income or success.

Score structured-form gold control and actual deterministic extraction. Live LLM
is a separately enabled command; absent inference remains unexecuted. Gold spans,
source/availability, polarity, unknown/conflict and review status are retained.
Exact seven-concept-plus-span matching supplies per-field/micro/macro scores.
Missing predictions are misses; unsupported-only empty outputs have their own
denominator. Report component, temporal, span, schema and quarantine counts,
per-style and per-scenario. No confidence calibration is claimed.

Matched snapshots compare all structured observables, exactly note-supported
gold through an explicitly documented canonical semantic bridge, actual extracted
prose, and missing notes. Hold feasible set, estimator and point allocator fixed.
Report belief/risk/ranking/assignment differences. An active-programme actual
session fixture checks later cancellation/allocation propagation. Its deliberately
selected boundary is engineering evidence, not population effectiveness evidence.
Closed-loop style and corruption arms render only their own authorized emissions.
Zero extraction attempts yield unavailable, never agreement.

## Secondary grid and scorecards

Primary runs first after bounded development feasibility. Secondary controls on
five held-out worlds: WAIT, random feasible, analytical-terminal-risk priority and
separately labeled trained-four-week-event-risk priority. Five information arms
hold FairPoint fixed: accurate structured-equivalent, random missing .30,
group-dependent, corrupted language, held-out style. Sensitivities hold primary
information fixed: low capacity 1, moderate 2, higher 3; zero, weak .25, moderate
.5 and default effect 1. Population sensitivity is separate. Reuse primary cells.
This is a bounded one-factor design, not a full Cartesian product. Delayed,
heterogeneous and adverse causal effects are unsupported by the current weekly
contract and are unexecuted, not silently invented.

Keep extraction, estimation/prediction, allocation and effectiveness scorecards
separate. Retention, withdrawal events, audited-group retention, offers,
completions, unique access, wait, normalized fairness deficits, comparable-need
access gaps, expenditure, utilization, solver statuses/incomplete stages and
extraction failures are raw world records. Paired Student-t 95% intervals use
independent episodes; students and landmarks never supply replicate counts.
Report raw means and paired differences, including worst-group retention and
access-gap deterioration. Small-group comparisons are descriptive/exploratory.

## Integrity and limits

Publish only into fresh sealed directories. A Phase 5 replay envelope records
full sampled profiles/lineage, source hashes, population/world seeds, model,
protocol/config, runtime, notes/extractions and complete weekly payload. Exact
replay regenerates source population and compares estimates, predictions,
decisions, fairness, resource ledger, draws and evaluator trajectory offline.
Historical v1/v2/v3 readers and runners remain unchanged. Matching archived
source/runtime is still required for historical exact execution.

Retention confidence intervals describe simulation noise conditional on the
chosen cohort/effects. Cohort sampling, effect assumptions, predictive error and
synthetic prose limitations remain separate. No invented dropout labels from
grades, causal inference from correlations, or test-driven model/prompt tuning.

## Dated non-vacuity amendment and retained predecessor

The v1 run is retained unchanged. All 30 v1 pairs had zero offers because its
12-profile cohort had no tutoring-compatible primary support need. Its zero
retention difference is unexercised, not a policy result or equivalence evidence.
The v1 source/configuration/data snapshot and verifier are separately archived.

A public source-context audit found only one source-test tutoring-compatible
anchor in the True disadvantage-proxy group, compared with 54 in False. Using
that attribute here cannot support a useful comparable-need group comparison.
The v2 protocol therefore uses the already supported source-sex audit attribute,
48 unstratified whole profiles, unchanged population seed 55000, and fresh
held-out world seeds 2000–2029. No source needs or eligibility rules change, and
no retention result was used to select profiles or a population seed. This is a
changed research population/group design, not a continuation of the v1 primary.

Before policy execution, every primary/development world must have feasible
new offers in both audited groups and at least one shared nonempty need stratum.
After execution, both primary methods must have made at least one actual offer.
Otherwise the run fails as unexercised rather than publishing a primary estimate.
The development pilot precedes the fresh held-out run; failure does not permit
silent resampling or choosing an alternative seed. The original model candidates,
training/validation rules and held-out language cases are unchanged. Reusing the
prediction test is a reproducibility check of the frozen procedure, not a fresh
independent model-selection opportunity.
